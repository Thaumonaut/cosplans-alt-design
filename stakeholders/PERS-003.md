## Identity

**Name / Archetype:** Rio — The Aspiring Creator

**Age:** Early 20s

**Gender:** Non-binary

**Location:** Mid-sized city, active online more than locally

**Life situation:** Works part-time retail and takes occasional commission work (props and accessories). Lives with roommates, wants to grow cosplay into a sustainable side business.

---

## Cosplay Context

**Primary context:** Emerging professional — turning cosplay skills into paid work.

**Experience:** 4–6 years cosplaying, highly skilled in one or two domains (e.g. foam armor, props). Has done a handful of paid commissions and small collaborations.

**Convention pattern:** Attends 2–3 cons a year, plus local meetups; uses events to network and show off builds that can attract future clients.

---

## Behaviours & Tech Profile

**Tech comfort:** High — very comfortable with social platforms and creator tools; uses planning tools inconsistently.

**Primary apps/tools:** Instagram, TikTok, Twitter/X for showcasing work; Google Sheets or Notion for tracking commissions and budgets; basic calendar app for deadlines.

**Cosplay information sources:** Other creators’ content, maker YouTube channels, Patreon/Ko-fi posts, Discord servers, and niche subreddits.

**Workarounds:** Tracks commissions, personal builds, and content ideas in separate places (notes, spreadsheets, DMs). Uses mental math and rough estimates for time and pricing, often underpricing work.

---

## Pain Points

1. Struggling to balance personal passion projects with paid commissions — everything competes for the same limited time.
2. Frequently underestimates how long builds and commissions will take, leading to underpricing and rushed delivery.
3. Has a backlog of half-baked ideas and WIPs that never become portfolio-worthy finished pieces.

---

## Motivations & Goals

**What they want:** A clearer roadmap from “cool idea” to “finished cosplay or commission” that accounts for time, materials, and deadlines, so they can price fairly and avoid burnout.

**What success looks like:** A small but steady stream of commissions they’re proud of, a polished portfolio that reflects their best work, and realistic planning that leaves room for personal builds.

**What they fear:** Burning out on cosplay by overcommitting to underpaid work, or never quite making the leap from “hobbyist with a Ko-fi” to “trusted creator people hire.”

---

## Quote
> "I know I can make amazing things — I just need a way to plan my builds so they’re actually finishable and worth what I charge."
