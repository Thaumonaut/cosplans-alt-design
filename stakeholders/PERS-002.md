## Identity

**Name / Archetype:** Jenna — The Overbooked Group Lead

**Age:** Late 20s

**Gender:** Woman

**Location:** Major US metro area, travels to regional cons

**Life situation:** Works full-time in a creative field, lives with a partner who is supportive but not a cosplayer. Runs a small cosplay group that regularly plans group builds and photoshoots across multiple conventions each year.

---

## Cosplay Context

**Primary context:** Group-focused organizer — leads multi-person cosplay projects and events.

**Experience:** 8+ years of cosplay experience; comfortable with complex builds and coordinating roles across a team.

**Convention pattern:** Attends 3–5 major cons a year plus local events, often with at least one large group cosplay per season.

---

## Behaviours & Tech Profile

**Tech comfort:** High — lives in Discord, Google Docs, and shared drives; quick to try new tools if they help coordination.

**Primary apps/tools:** Discord servers for group chat, Google Sheets for budgets, shared Google Drive folders for reference images, Trello/Notion boards for task tracking (inconsistently maintained).

**Cosplay information sources:** Instagram, TikTok, YouTube tutorials, private Discord servers, and shared Pinterest boards.

**Workarounds:** Juggles multiple overlapping tools for each group (chat in Discord, references in Drive, tasks in Trello), leading to confusion about where the “real plan” lives. Often rebuilds planning docs from scratch for each new group cosplay.

---

## Pain Points

1. Keeping everyone aligned on which version of the plan is current (tasks, deadlines, budgets, and reference visuals).
2. Juggling timelines across several group projects and events at once without a clear view of what’s realistic.
3. Spending too much time on coordination overhead instead of actually building costumes.

---

## Motivations & Goals

**What they want:** A central hub where ideas, visual references, tasks, and budgets for each group project live together, so everyone can see what needs doing and when.

**What success looks like:** Group cosplays are planned with realistic timelines, fewer last-minute scrambles, and clear ownership; her friends show up to cons feeling prepared and proud of their builds.

**What they fear:** Dropping the ball on a group build, causing stress or disappointment for friends, or leading teams into overly ambitious projects they can’t finish.

---

## Quote
> "I can wrangle a group into doing almost anything… if I’m not lost in ten different spreadsheets and Discord channels first."
