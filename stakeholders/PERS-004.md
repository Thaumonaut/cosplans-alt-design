## Identity

**Name / Archetype:** Sam — The Overwhelmed Newcomer

**Age:** Late teens to early 20s

**Gender:** Any / unspecified

**Location:** Suburban area, first big cons within driving distance

**Life situation:** Student or early in their career, limited disposable income, living with family or roommates. Has loved cosplay from afar for years and is finally trying to make their first few serious builds.

---

## Cosplay Context

**Primary context:** Newer cosplayer — high enthusiasm, low experience.

**Experience:** 0–2 serious cosplays completed; many Pinterest boards and saved TikToks but little structured planning.

**Convention pattern:** Has attended one or two cons, wants to go more; dreams of debuting specific characters but feels intimidated by the process.

---

## Behaviours & Tech Profile

**Tech comfort:** High in social apps, medium in productivity tools — very comfortable on TikTok, Instagram, Discord, but less experienced with structured planning tools.

**Primary apps/tools:** Pinterest boards, TikTok “favorites,” Instagram saves, screenshots in camera roll, basic phone notes.

**Cosplay information sources:** Popular creators on TikTok and YouTube, fandom discords, friends who have cosplayed longer.

**Workarounds:** Juggles multiple moodboards and saved collections with no single source of truth. Starts lists in notebooks or notes apps but quickly abandons them when overwhelmed.

---

## Pain Points

1. Has too many ideas and inspirations but no clear way to turn them into a realistic first (or second) full cosplay project.
2. Easily overwhelmed by tutorials, material choices, and budgeting, leading to procrastination and half-started attempts.
3. Feels intimidated by more experienced cosplayers’ polished builds and social media presence.

---

## Motivations & Goals

**What they want:** A gentle, visual way to gather all their ideas in one place and gradually turn them into a concrete plan they can actually follow.

**What success looks like:** Successfully completing a cosplay they’re proud to wear at a con, with enough structure that they don’t feel lost at each step — and without blowing their student/early-career budget.

**What they fear:** Spending money on the wrong materials, getting stuck halfway through a build, or feeling like they “don’t belong” in the cosplay community because their work isn’t polished enough.

---

## Quote
> "I have so many cosplay ideas saved… I just don’t know how to start without messing it up."
