## Identity

**Name / Archetype:** Michael — The Regular

**Age:** Mid-20s

**Gender:** Man (assumed; adjust if needed)

**Location:** Near Portland, OR, USA

**Life situation:** Works full-time as a salaried office worker at a local corporation. Single, lives with two cats and a high school friend as a roommate. Weekdays are mostly consumed by work, with evenings and weekends shared between cosplay, social time with his roommate, and trying to date.

---

## Cosplay Context

**Primary context:** Long-time hobbyist — has been cosplaying for years and regularly attends conventions and meetups.

**Experience:** Comfortable with the basics of planning and building costumes, but still refining his process for juggling multiple projects and deadlines.

**Convention pattern:** Attends several cons and local events each year, often aiming to debut at least one new cosplay per major event.

---

## Behaviours & Tech Profile

**Tech comfort:** Medium — uses specific apps heavily (messaging, social media, notes), and is open to new tools if they clearly help him stay organized.

**Primary apps/tools:** Notes app on his phone, messaging apps where he DMs himself links, and social platforms like Instagram and TikTok for cosplay inspiration.

**Cosplay information sources:** Instagram reels, TikTok videos, and posts from other cosplayers form most of his idea stream, with occasional reference images saved from the web.

**Workarounds:** Saves ideas and references in scattered notes and self-messages. Keeps track of builds mostly in his head and via ad hoc lists, leading to scrambled con crunch and forgotten ideas.

---

## Pain Points

1. Feels like he never has enough time for cosplay because work and life take most of his energy, so builds often get pushed into last-minute con crunch.
2. Frequently ends up rushing to finish costumes before events and is rarely fully happy with the final result because corners had to be cut under time pressure.
3. Struggles to balance cosplay with hanging out with his roommate, caring for his cats, and going on dates — when free time is limited, he doesn’t have a clear plan for what to work on.

---

## Motivations & Goals

**What he wants:** To feel in control of his cosplay projects — with a realistic plan that fits around his job and social life, instead of constantly reacting to looming deadlines.

**What success looks like:** He arrives at cons with finished, well-crafted cosplays he’s proud of, without burning himself out or sacrificing all his weekends and social time to do it.

**What he fears:** Overcommitting to ambitious builds and ending up with rushed, half-finished costumes (or bailing on them entirely), which makes him feel like he’s wasting both time and money.

---

## Quote
> "I just want to actually finish my cosplays without killing all my free time right before a con."
