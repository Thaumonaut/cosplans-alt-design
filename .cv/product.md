## Identity

**Name:** Cosplans

**Type:** Cross-platform web application (web today, mobile app later)

**Stage:** Live, early development (MVP live, limited users, incomplete and buggy)

**One-line description:** Cosplans is an ADHD-friendly planning studio for cosplayers that turns scattered inspiration into moodboards, plans, and projects — from first spark to finished cosplay.

---

## What It Does

**Core user action:** Cosplayers capture and organize every idea for future builds into a visual moodboard workspace, then shape those ideas into concrete projects with plans, budgets, and support.

**Planning assistant role (AI):** A quiet, opt-in planning assistant helps break down builds into tasks, suggest timelines and budgets, and surface “what should I do next?” prompts — it supports planning and decision-making, but does not generate creative assets or edit media.

**Key features:** *(high-level, not exhaustive)*

- Visual moodboards that feel like a cross between Pinterest and Milanote, tailored to cosplay.
- Idea-to-project flows that turn boards of inspiration into trackable builds with tasks, budgets, and events.
- Early marketplace hooks to find adjacent collaborators (wig stylists, prop makers, MUAs, photographers, seamstresses, event coordinators).
- ADHD-friendly design touches: piles, templates, “what should I do now?” prompts, and clear milestones to reduce overwhelm.

---

## Target Context

**Primary platform:** Mixed — web for deep planning sessions, PWA/mobile share flow for fast capture.

**Typical usage context:** Users quickly save inspiration from social apps or the web into Cosplans on their phone (via share/PWA), then later sit down at a computer or tablet to clean up boards, plan builds, and manage projects before cons or photoshoots.

**Target user:** Cosplayers at all levels — from solo hobbyists dreaming up their first build to small groups and emerging professionals who need a central place to plan, track, and eventually sell or collaborate. (See personas in `.cv/spec.md` for Solo Cosplayer, Cosplay Group, Creator/Commissioner, Professional.)

---

## Tone and Voice

**Voice:** Warm, encouraging hype-friend — motivational without pressure.

**Communication principles:**

- Speaks like a fellow cosplayer who’s excited about your ideas, not a productivity guru.
- Reduces shame and pressure — celebrates small progress and unfinished works-in-progress instead of scolding about backlog.
- Focuses on possibility and next steps: “Here’s one small thing you can do now,” not “You’re behind.”
- Uses plain, accessible language with light fandom flavor where appropriate, not corporate or clinical jargon.

---

## Differentiators

- Starts at **ideation**, not just project setup — a home for every “someday I’ll make this” idea, even if it never becomes a formal project.
- Combines **visual moodboards and practical project management** in one place, tuned specifically for cosplay workflows and events.
- Built **ADHD-first**, with piles, templates, and “what should I do now?” guidance designed to combat paralysis, not just organize tasks.
- Designed to **grow into a marketplace and support network**, helping cosplayers find the wig stylists, prop makers, photographers, and other collaborators they need.

---

## What This Product Does Not Do

- Does **not** generate or edit creative assets (no AI image editing, no “fix this cosplay photo” or auto-designing costumes).
- Does **not** aim to replace human creativity or the joy of building; it supports planning and coordination, not the creative act itself.
- Does **not** position itself as “an AI product” — the assistant is a background planning helper, not the main experience.
- Does **not** attempt to be a generic project management tool; it stays focused on cosplay-specific workflows, language, and events.
